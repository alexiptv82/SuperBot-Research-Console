      otherwise RETRYABLE_PRECONDITION_FAILURE. The status is reported
      as A010_RECOVERY_STATUS=<the code of the first finding of the
      record's CLASS>.
  (d) Authority. The NEW Step-4 author never classifies and never makes
      a classification record. The classifying party is the party
      performing the step or gate at which the failure is detected: the
      independent verifier or auditor of A010-R2 to A010-R6, the Section
      9.4 verifier, the Section 8.11(b) verifier, the A010-R8 verifier,
      the party performing A008 §16 step 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9 (for step 8,
      also the Section 9.7(e)(iv) verifier), or, for a failure in its
      own base, surface, supply or checkpoint acts, the session
      operator. In addition:
        - TERMINAL: made by the classifying party or by any independent
          auditor or verifier of the A010 recovery lineage that
          establishes a terminal predicate. A report by the NEW Step-4
          author of receipt of, access to or inheritance of content not
          permitted by Section 8.4 is classified
          STOP_CLEAN_ROOM_INPUT_VIOLATION by the Section 9.4 verifier
          upon receipt;
        - RETRYABLE: made by the classifying party, whose record states
          the phase and the facts establishing each element of the
          code's predicate;
        - REMEDIABLE: made by the classifying party, which satisfies
          Section 5.2 with respect to the NEW implementation.
  (e) CLASSIFICATION RECORD. Every classification is made in an external
      classification record, created before any corrective act, UTF-8
      with LF line endings, containing exactly these members in this
      order:
        FORMAT_VERSION=A010_CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_V1
        CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_ID=<Section 14.10(b)>
        CLASS=<a class of (a)>
        DETECTION_POINT=<the A010-Rn step, the A008 §16 step or the gate
          P-02 or P-03 at which the failure was detected>
        PHASE=PRE_SUPPLY|POST_SUPPLY
        STEP4_SESSION_ID=<value>|NONE
        REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID=<Section 14.10(e)-(f)>|NONE
        AUDITED_COMMIT=<lowercase 40-hex implementation checkpoint>|NONE
        CLASSIFIER=<identifier of the classifying party's context>
        CORRECTIVE_ACTION=<the Section 14.5(c) action>|NONE
        FINDING_COUNT=<decimal integer, at least 1>
      then, for each finding:
        FINDING_ID=<Section 14.10(d)>
        FINDING_CODE=<a code of Section 14.3(b), 14.4(b) or 14.5(b)>
        PREDICATE_CLAUSE=<the clause(s) whose complete predicate is

        EVIDENCE=<identities of the records, commits and paths relied

      A record of class REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_FAILURE states an
      AUDITED_COMMIT and cites Section 14.4(a) among its
      PREDICATE_CLAUSE lines; a record of class
      RETRYABLE_PRECONDITION_FAILURE states a CORRECTIVE_ACTION other
      than NONE. The record of a rerun gate names the
      CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_ID to which it responds. A non-PASS A010-R1
      verdict is fully recorded by its A010-R1 audit record (Section
      5.3(d)) and receives no classification record.
  (f) No reclassification. A classification record is never edited or
      withdrawn, and no failure is ever moved to a lower class. If a
      terminal predicate is later established for a failure recorded as
      RETRYABLE or REMEDIABLE, a new classification record of class
      TERMINAL_NORMATIVE_STOP naming the earlier record is made; this
      escalation is mandatory and is the only change of class.

14.3 TERMINAL_NORMATIVE_STOP
  (a) Criterion. Continuation would require:
        (i) changing frozen normative meaning;
       (ii) accepting altered protected protocol bytes;
      (iii) overriding a failed required normative audit whose
            remediation cannot occur under the existing authorization;
       (iv) accepting an irreparable provenance or lineage
            contradiction; or
        (v) making a normative choice not authorized by A010.
  (b) Codes, in listed order (Section 14.2(c)):
      STOP_A008_BYTES_MISMATCH
        the committed raw bytes at A008_DRAFT4_PATH, at the commit from
        which A010-R3 reads them, differ from 98002 bytes /
        c78d6c25abcc11fe8164a83461c07dd98cf966fba6e35d5fa384910fd1672972.
      STOP_A008_REAUDIT_NOT_PASS
        the A010-R3 final provenance-only re-audit does not PASS.
      STOP_A008_FREEZE_BYTES_MISMATCH
        the A008 freeze contains bytes different from the audited bytes.
      STOP_A008_FREEZE_IDENTITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED
        the real A008 external freeze quadruple cannot be established or
        verified (Sections 5.6(e), 5.7(a)-(c), 7.5).
      STOP_A009_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
        the committed A009 frozen bytes differ from its external
        identity, or the external record differs from Section 1.3.
      STOP_A010_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
        the committed A010 frozen bytes differ from its external identity
        or from the A010-R1-audited bytes.
      STOP_LINEAGE_VIOLATION
        a relation L0-L4 of Section 7.3 fails, a constraint of Section
        7.4(a)-(c) fails, or the authoritative lineage has been
        rewritten (Section 7.4(d)).
      STOP_COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT_NOT_PASS
        A010-R6 does not PASS on a ground not assigned to another code.
      STOP_CLEAN_ROOM_INPUT_VIOLATION
        POST_SUPPLY, for a NEW session: content not permitted by Section
        8.4 was supplied to, or was readable by, its authoring context,
        including by a nonconformance of its CLOSED AUTHOR SURFACE to
        Section 9.3, through any access channel or through its generic
        platform layer (Section 9.3(i)); an ineligible context
        (Section 8.3) acted as the NEW author; content was supplied to
        an authoring context without a conforming Section 8.9 record and
        a Section 9.4 PASS record; a message other than a Section 9.3(c)
        control message was delivered to it; a finding reached the NEW
        Step-4 author otherwise than as a conforming SRR, or a
        non-conforming SRR was supplied (Section 8.10(h)); output of
        another NEW session was supplied to it or readable by it
        (Section 8.11(c)(i)); or the NEW author's attestation, or any
        report by it, declares receipt of, access to or inheritance of
        content not permitted by Section 8.4, or names as received a
        record for which no conforming supply-log entry exists.
      STOP_SESSION_BASE_INVALID
        POST_SUPPLY: the STEP4_SESSION_BASE_COMMIT of the NEW session
        fails a condition of Section 9.2 other than relation L3.
      STOP_CIRCULAR_IDENTITY_DEPENDENCY
        the frozen protocol texts or the identity model of Section 10.5
        would require an identity cycle.
      STOP_PROTOCOL_IDENTITY_UNVERIFIABLE
        an external freeze record of A008, A009 or A010 is missing,
        contradictory or not exactly verifiable under Section 6.4, so
        that no conforming implementation could satisfy P-02 and P-03.
      STOP_REMEDIATION_NOT_AVAILABLE
        a failure that would otherwise be remediable cannot be remediated
        under Section 14.4, including because the current NEW session
        is unavailable when a remediation of it is required (Sections
        8.11(d), 14.4(j)); or, under the no-replacement branch of Section
        8.11(d), a still-uncleared RETRYABLE finding concerning the
        unavailable session has an ORIGINAL_TARGET that Section 14.5(d)(7)(ii)
        establishes requires an act, delivery, output, work-product change,
        new implementation checkpoint or remediation by that unavailable
        session and therefore cannot be performed; continuation would require
        an unpermitted replacement or clearance without satisfying the target
        and therefore meets the Section 14.3(a)(v) criterion.
      STOP_UNCLASSIFIED_FAILURE
        a failure within A010_RECOVERY_FAILURE_SCOPE for which the
        Section 14.3(a) criterion holds, or no other code of Sections
        14.3-14.5 is established (Section 14.2(c)); never an underlying
        A008/A009 outcome whose root cause lies outside that scope
        (Section 14.1(d)).
  (c) A TERMINAL_NORMATIVE_STOP MUST NOT be cleared by retry,
      reinterpretation or reclassification, nor by the party that
      detected it. Continuation requires a new independently audited and
      exact-byte-frozen amendment.

14.4 REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_FAILURE
  (a) Conditions. Either (1) the defect lies only in the NEW Step-4
      implementation (the seven A009 §12.1 files authored in the current
      NEW session) or in an artifact produced from it at A008 §16 step
      6, and its correction requires edits only to those seven files
      (the remediation edit targets of (f)); or (2) the failure is an
      unauthorized seven-path change (Section 9.7(g)(2)), whose
      correction requires only a new implementation checkpoint of the
      current NEW session. In either case the correction also requires
      the repetition of the steps of (e), whose writing of the derived
      orchestration artifact is not such an edit, and requires no change
      to any frozen protocol byte, to frozen normative meaning, to any
      Reference byte or A008 §15.2 protected path, and no normative
      choice not authorized by A010. Nothing is consumed.
  (b) Codes, in listed order (Section 14.2(c)):
      REMEDIABLE_UNAUTHORIZED_SEVEN_PATH_CHANGE
        at a commit stated for a step of a pass, the seven-path bytes
        differ from the PASS implementation binding, or a seven path was
        added, modified or deleted after the PASS implementation
        checkpoint (Section 9.7(e)(2)-(3), (g)(2)), and no terminal
        predicate holds.
      REMEDIABLE_EXPECTED_IDENTITY_SOURCE_DEFECT
        the implementation derives an expected protocol identity from
        the implementation freeze manifest or from another source
        excluded by Section 13.1(b).
      REMEDIABLE_CIRCULAR_IDENTITY_DEPENDENCY
        the implementation introduces an identity-dependency cycle not
        required by the frozen texts.
      REMEDIABLE_REQUIRED_FILE_ABSENT
        after a completion declaration, a path of A009 §12.1 is absent
        in the tree of the implementation checkpoint audited (Sections
        5.10(a), 13.1(d)).
      REMEDIABLE_ATTESTATION_DEFECT
        the attestation at the implementation checkpoint audited lacks,
        misstates or malforms content required by A008 §5.3, A009 §8.3
        or Section 8.8, or is inconsistent with a record named in
        Section 5.10(d), and no terminal predicate holds.
      REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT
        any other defect meeting (a)(1), detected at A010-R8, at A008 §16
        step 5 (including Section 14.6(c)-(d)), at A008 §16 step 6
        (including a seven-file edit that an A008 §3.3.5 repetition is
        established to require, Section 14.1(e)), by the A008 §16 step-7
        audit, or at A008 §16 step 9.
  (c) Classification and records. A failure detected by a party other
      than the NEW Step-4 author is classified under Section 14.2; a
      classification record of class REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_FAILURE
      opens a remediation loop (Section 14.10(e)) and authorizes the
      remediation, and its findings reach the NEW Step-4 author only as
      SRRs (Section 8.10). A failure that the NEW Step-4 author observes
      in a synthetic test it runs itself within its CLOSED AUTHOR
      SURFACE (Section 9.3(h)), after a completion declaration and
      before A008 §16 step 6 of the current pass (Section 14.10(e))
      begins, is reported by it and corrected by it without a
      classification record; before that step 6 begins, the session
      operator records the report as a self-detected remediation-log
      entry (Section 14.10(g)), which opens a remediation loop; such
      entries are reviewed under Section 13.1(h). A report made after
      step 6 of the current pass has begun is classified under Section
      14.2 by the party performing the current step. A finding of code
      REMEDIABLE_UNAUTHORIZED_SEVEN_PATH_CHANGE is corrected under
      Section 9.7(g)(2), without an SRR and without an edit by the NEW
      Step-4 author.
  (d) Performer and constraints. Only the current NEW session performs
      the remediation, and only within Sections 8 and 9 (in particular
      Sections 8.2-8.7, 8.10, 9.3 and 9.5), A008 §5 and A009 §§8.3-8.4.
      No other party edits the seven files (Section 9.7(f)); the session
      operator's byte-for-byte transfer at an implementation checkpoint
      is not an edit. STEP4_SESSION_BASE_COMMIT and the Section 9
      isolation of that session are not changed, and steps A010-R1 to
      A010-R6 are not repeated.
  (e) Steps repeated and attribution. For this subsection and Section
      14.10(e), the phrases "before the implementation freeze of A008
      §16 step 8" and "after the implementation freeze of A008 §16
      step 8" are relative to the CURRENT PASS in which the finding is
      detected. BEFORE means that no successful step-8 freeze record yet
      exists for that pass; AFTER means that such a record exists. A
      finding detected during the step-8 freeze act or by the independent
      Section 9.7(e)(iv) verifier, where that finding prevents successful
      completion of step 8, is a BEFORE-freeze detection and has step 8
      as its clearance target.
      (1) Return before A008 §16 step 8 (A010-originated). For an
          opening failure or report arising before successful step 8 of
          the current pass: specifically, a failure detected at A010-R8,
          at A008 §16 step 5, at step 6 (an A008 §3.3.5 repetition that
          requires no seven-file edit remaining governed by A008 §3.3.5

          finding of code REMEDIABLE_UNAUTHORIZED_SEVEN_PATH_CHANGE, a

14.5 RETRYABLE_PRECONDITION_FAILURE
  (a) Conditions. A gate fails, or cannot be completed, solely because
      of a working-tree, workspace, session-base designation or
      transient state discrepancy, or, for
      RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8, solely because of a
      NEW-session unavailability under Section 8.11(c), while (i) the
      committed bytes at every relevant freeze and basis commit and
      every external freeze or audit record remain intact and valid,
      (ii) protocol meaning is unchanged, and (iii) no source not
      permitted by Section 8.4 has been supplied to, or become readable
      by, a NEW Step-4 authoring context that has received any Section
      8.4 input.
  (b) Codes, in listed order (Section 14.2(c)):
      RETRY_WORKING_TREE_MISMATCH
        working-tree raw bytes at a protected protocol path differ from
        the committed bytes (for example by line-ending conversion or a
        checkout filter) while the committed bytes satisfy the relevant
        identity (Sections 5.4(d), 5.5(b), 5.7, 5.8, 6.4(d)); or a
        working-tree seven-path byte set stated for an execution of a
        pass differs from its PASS implementation binding while Section
        9.7(e)(1)-(3) hold (Section 9.7(g)(3)).
      RETRY_WORKSPACE_STATE_MISMATCH
        PRE_SUPPLY only: the Section 9.4 verification finds the CLOSED
        AUTHOR SURFACE, its surface manifest, its session parameter
        record, its access configuration or its generic platform layer
        not conforming to Section 9.3, or the generic-only condition of
        Section 9.3(i) not established, for an authoring context that
        has received nothing.
      RETRY_SESSION_START_RECORD_NOT_ESTABLISHED
        PRE_SUPPLY only: a conforming Section 8.9 record cannot be
        established before A010-R7.2 for a context that has received
        nothing.
      RETRY_SESSION_BASE_NOT_CONFORMING
        PRE_SUPPLY only: the Section 9.4 verification finds that the
        designated STEP4_SESSION_BASE_COMMIT fails a condition of
        Section 9.2 other than relation L3.
      RETRY_TRANSIENT_UNAVAILABILITY
        a gate could not be carried to a verdict, or a delivery to or an
        output from an authoring context could not be made, because the
        repository, the platform, the authoring context or an intact
        external record was temporarily unreachable.
      RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8
        a NEW session is unavailable under the conditions of Section
        8.11(c).
  (c) Permitted corrective action, only: restoring working-tree bytes
      from the committed bytes (for example by a fresh byte-exact
      checkout); rebuilding a CLOSED AUTHOR SURFACE, its access
      configuration or its platform configuration before any supply;
      establishing a conforming Section 8.9 record for a fresh context
      that has received nothing;
      designating a conforming STEP4_SESSION_BASE_COMMIT reached by
      further ordinary forward commits under Section 9.2, the
      non-conforming commit never being designated; starting a
      replacement NEW session under Section 8.11(c); or awaiting
      availability. No protocol byte, frozen byte, committed history,
      external freeze record or audit verdict is edited, and no commit
      is created to correct a retryable failure other than the ordinary
      forward commits of Section 9.2 for RETRY_SESSION_BASE_NOT_CONFORMING
      and RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8.
  (d) Rerun and clearance. The failed gate is rerun from its beginning,
      in full, by a party eligible for it; no partial result of the failed
      attempt is reused. The clearance target of every RETRYABLE code is
      deterministic:
        (1) RETRY_WORKING_TREE_MISMATCH clears when the full rerun of the
            A010-Rn gate or verification, or A008 §16 step, at which it
            was detected PASSes with the relevant working-tree bytes
            conforming. Under Section
            9.7(g)(3), that rerun begins at the step of the execution
            concerned and every later step already performed in the pass
            is performed again.
        (2) RETRY_WORKSPACE_STATE_MISMATCH,
            RETRY_SESSION_START_RECORD_NOT_ESTABLISHED and
            RETRY_SESSION_BASE_NOT_CONFORMING clear only when the
            corrective action of (c) has been completed and the complete
            Section 9.4 verification PASSes.
        (3) RETRY_TRANSIENT_UNAVAILABILITY clears when the exact gate,
            delivery or output operation whose temporary unavailability
            caused the code is successfully completed; if it is a gate,
            the complete rerun of that gate must PASS. If the authoring
            context whose act is required by that clearance target is later
            established UNAVAILABLE under Section 8.11(b) before the act can
            complete, and the finding therefore concerns that session under
            Section 8.11(b), then: if Section 8.11(c) applies and a
            replacement is started, its clearance target is replaced by (6);
            if Section 8.11(d) applies, it is governed by (7). It is never
            left bound to an impossible act of the unavailable session.
        (4) RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8 clears only when the
            latest replacement NEW session started under Section 8.11(c),
            which is the CURRENT NEW SESSION of Section 8.11(e), has
            completed A010-R7.1 through A010-R7.3 and a conforming
            implementation checkpoint of that current replacement session
            states COMPLETION_DECLARED=YES. If that replacement itself
            becomes unavailable and a later replacement is permitted under
            Section 8.11(c), the clearance target advances to the later
            CURRENT NEW SESSION; neither the unavailability, the start of
            any replacement nor supersession alone clears the finding.
            A010-R8 is not needed to clear this code and may be attempted
            only after that clearance.
        (5) A RETRYABLE finding at A010-R8 clears when the complete A010-R8
            rerun PASSes. The RETRYABLE failure of A010-R8 itself does not
            bar that rerun under Section 5.10(e)(ii); the exception permits
            the rerun only and does not itself clear the failure. If the
            checkpoint and rerun belong to a NEW session later established
            UNAVAILABLE before the rerun can PASS, the finding concerns that
            session under Section 8.11(b). If Section 8.11(c) applies and a
            replacement is started, it is governed by (6); if Section 8.11(d)
            applies, it is governed by (7).
        (6) REPLACEMENT-CHAIN CLEARANCE. When a verified Section 8.11(b)
            SESSION UNAVAILABILITY RECORD for S is followed by a replacement
            under Section 8.11(c), every still-uncleared RETRYABLE finding
            concerning S and listed in that record is carried without
            reclassification and clears exactly when the
            RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8 finding recorded for S
            clears under (4). This rule changes only its clearance target;
            its original classification record, FINDING_ID and FINDING_CODE
            remain immutable. It includes, where applicable, a
            RETRY_TRANSIENT_UNAVAILABILITY for a delivery to or output from
            S, an earlier RETRY_NEW_SESSION_UNAVAILABLE_BEFORE_R8 whose
            current replacement session was S, and any RETRYABLE finding at
            A010-R8 for a checkpoint of S. A RETRYABLE finding that does not
            concern S under Section 8.11(b) retains its original clearance
            target and is not cleared by replacement. A later-established
            terminal predicate always prevails under Sections 14.2(f) and
            14.3.
        (7) NO-REPLACEMENT SESSION-LOSS DISPOSITION. When a verified Section
            8.11(b) SESSION UNAVAILABILITY RECORD for S falls under Section
            8.11(d), every still-uncleared RETRYABLE finding concerning S and
            listed in that record is handled without reclassification as
            follows. Let ORIGINAL_TARGET be that finding's current clearance
            target immediately before S became UNAVAILABLE.
              (i) If ORIGINAL_TARGET can be completed without any new act,
                  delivery, output, work-product change or implementation
                  checkpoint of S, the finding retains ORIGINAL_TARGET and
                  clears only when the complete rerun or operation required
                  by that target PASSes. In particular, an A010-R8 RETRYABLE
                  finding for an already-existing conforming checkpoint of S
                  may retain the A010-R8 rerun as its target when that rerun
                  requires no new act of S and no remediation or new
                  checkpoint.
             (ii) If ORIGINAL_TARGET requires any act, delivery, output,
                  work-product change, new implementation checkpoint or
                  remediation by S, that target is impossible once S is
                  UNAVAILABLE and STOP_REMEDIATION_NOT_AVAILABLE is
                  established under Section 14.3 because continuation would
                  require an unpermitted replacement or clearance without the
                  required target PASS (Section 14.3(a)(v)). The RETRYABLE
                  finding is not thereby cleared; terminal precedence under Sections
                  14.2(f), 14.3 and 14.8 ends further retry effects.

14.6 SYNTHETIC-TEST OUTCOMES
  (a) Every fixture required by Section 13.2 or A009 §14.2 asserts, in
      the hash-bound test source, its expected outcome: success, or a
      specified failure such as PRECONDITION_FAIL.
  (b) EXPECTED_NEGATIVE_TEST_RESULT. A PRECONDITION_FAIL produced by a
      deliberately malformed negative fixture whose asserted expected
      outcome it is, is a successful synthetic-test result. It is not a
      failure of any class, not a runtime or recovery failure, and does
      not make the implementation unfreezable.
  (c) An unexpected PRECONDITION_FAIL on the conforming positive
      fixture, on real-identity verification, or on any other fixture
      whose expected outcome is success, is a test failure and a
      REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT, unless Section 14.3 applies.
  (d) A negative fixture that does not produce its required
      PRECONDITION_FAIL, including one that succeeds or produces an
      outcome other than the asserted one, is itself a test failure and
      a REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT.
  (e) The implementation is not freezable at A008 §16 step 8 until every
      required fixture produces exactly its asserted expected outcome.

14.10 RECORD IDENTIFIERS AND REMEDIATION LOOPS
  (a) Lineage and index. The A010 RECOVERY LINEAGE is the set of
      external records made under A010 for the authoritative branch of
      Section 0.9(a). The A010 RECORD INDEX is an external, append-only
      list, kept in that lineage, of every CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_ID and
      SRR_ID assigned, each with its record type, raw size and SHA256, in
      assignment order. An identifier is assigned only by entering it in
      the index.
  (b) CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_ID is assigned by the party creating the
      classification record, exactly as A010-CR-<k>, where <k> is the
      decimal value of one plus the number of CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_IDs
      already in the index. It is unique in the A010 recovery lineage
      and never reused.
  (c) SRR_ID is assigned by the SRR producer, exactly as A010-SRR-<k>,
      where <k> is the decimal value of one plus the number of SRR_IDs
      already in the index. It is entered in the index, with the SRR's
      raw size and SHA256, before the Section 8.10(e) verification and
      before supply; it is unique in the A010 recovery lineage and never
      reused.
  (d) FINDING_ID is exactly <CLASSIFICATION_RECORD_ID>-F<j>, where <j> is
      the decimal position (1 to FINDING_COUNT) of the finding in its
      classification record. It identifies exactly one finding and is
      therefore unique in the lineage. An SRR repeats unchanged the
      FINDING_ID of each finding it carries.
  (e) REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID is a positive decimal integer scoped to one
      STEP4_SESSION_ID and never reused within it.
        Begin. Loop n of session S begins when its OPENING RECORD is
        recorded: a classification record of class
        REMEDIABLE_IMPLEMENTATION_FAILURE concerning S, or a
        self-detected remediation-log entry concerning S ((g)). The
        party making the opening record sets n to one plus the greatest
        REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID previously recorded for S, or to 1 if there
        is none, and states n in that record. The opening failure or
        self-detected report, and every finding contained in a
        classification opening record, are ASSIGNED TO loop n without
        reclassification. The opening record ends the preceding pass at
        that instant. Every nonterminal finding of that preceding pass,
        or of an earlier superseded loop, that is still uncleared at that
        instant is CARRIED into loop n without changing its class, code,
        evidence or clearance target. A terminal finding
        is never carried because it terminates the sequence.
        Scope. The BEFORE/AFTER step-8 phase is the pass-relative phase
        defined by Section 14.4(e). The BASE REPEATED SEQUENCE of loop n
        is: through A008 §16 step 7 for an opening failure detected at
        A010-R8 or steps 5-7 before successful step 8 of its pass, and for
        a self-detected report under Section 14.4(c); through A008 §16
        step 8 when the opening failure is detected during step 8 and
        prevents successful freeze; and through A008 §16 step 9 for an
        opening failure detected after successful step 8. Each carried
        nonterminal finding retains the clearance target fixed by Section
        14.4(g) or 14.5(d). For carried findings whose target is within
        the implementation sequence, order those targets as A010-R8,
        step 5, step 6, step 7, step 8, step 9. A RETRYABLE finding whose
        clearance target precedes A010-R8 remains governed by Section
        14.5(d) and must clear before A010-R8 under Section 5.10(e); it
        cannot extend the loop sequence. The EFFECTIVE REPEATED SEQUENCE
        of loop n is its base sequence extended through the latest target,
        in that order, of every carried finding that remains uncleared. It never
        extends beyond step 9. Such extension is clearance bookkeeping
        only: it does not reclassify any finding, create a new normative
        authorization or create a new A008 §16.3 trigger. The sequence from
        the first A010-R8 of S up to the first opening record is the
        INITIAL PASS; the initial pass and each loop are each a PASS. The
        pass of loop n begins at its opening record and continues, after
        loop n ends COMPLETED, through the later A008 §16 steps performed
        on its implementation; every pass ends at the next opening record
        concerning S or at a TERMINAL_NORMATIVE_STOP. A pass contains at
        most one A010-R8 PASS and has at most one PASS implementation
        binding (Section 9.7(b)).
        End. Loop n ends at exactly the first of: COMPLETED, when the last
        step of its EFFECTIVE REPEATED SEQUENCE PASSes and every
        nonterminal finding assigned to or carried by the loop whose
        clearance target lies in that sequence has cleared under Section
        14.4(g) or 14.5(d); SUPERSEDED, when a further opening record
        concerning S is recorded, which begins loop n+1 at the same
        instant and carries every still-uncleared nonterminal finding
        forward under Scope above; TERMINATED, when a classification
        record of class TERMINAL_NORMATIVE_STOP is recorded. The record of
        the ending event states that loop n has ended and which of these
        ends applies. At most one loop of S is open at any time.
        Supersession never clears a finding by itself. A carried
        REMEDIABLE finding clears only under Section 14.4(g), and a carried
        RETRYABLE finding only under Section 14.5(d).
  (f) Binding. Every SRR, classification record, self-detected
      remediation-log entry, checkpoint record, PASS implementation
      binding record, and record of A010-R8 and of A008 §16 steps 5-9,
      concerning S and made while loop n of S is
      open, states STEP4_SESSION_ID=S and REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID=n, except
      that an opening record states the loop it opens; such a record
      made while no loop of S is open states REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID=NONE.
      A record not concerning any NEW session states STEP4_SESSION_ID=
      NONE and REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID=NONE.
  (g) SELF-DETECTED REMEDIATION LOG. On a report by the NEW Step-4 author
      under Section 14.4(c), the session operator makes an external log
      entry, UTF-8 with LF line endings, containing exactly these
      members in this order:
        FORMAT_VERSION=A010_SELF_DETECTED_REMEDIATION_LOG_ENTRY_V1
        STEP4_SESSION_ID=<value>
        REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID=<the loop it opens>
        OBSERVED_AT_CHECKPOINT=<lowercase 40-hex implementation
          checkpoint whose seven-path content the report concerns>
        TEST_IDENTIFIER=<the test identifier reported>
        ASSERTED_EXPECTED_OUTCOME=<the outcome asserted in the test
          source>
        OBSERVED_OUTCOME=<the outcome reported>
      The entry is identified by the pair (STEP4_SESSION_ID,
      REMEDIATION_LOOP_ID). It is not a classification record and has

  (h) No identifier other than those of (b)-(e) is introduced by this
