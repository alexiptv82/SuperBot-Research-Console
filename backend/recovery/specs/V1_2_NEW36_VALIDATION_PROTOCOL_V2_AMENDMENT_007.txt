======================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
======================================================================

1.1 Clean-room Reference authoring exposed a possible logical
    inconsistency between frozen V2 invariants. A targeted consistency
    audit (Section 2) confirmed that V2 A20_I12, and the equivalent
    Candidate-specification invariant I_HB_I12, are NOT logically
    entailed by the frozen B0, B1 and exact-spacing definitions, and
    can be violated by implementations that conform exactly to those
    definitions.

1.2 A007 does exactly one thing: it withdraws A20_I12 and I_HB_I12 as
    requirements for this V2 validation. It changes nothing else.

======================================================================
2. FINDING (NON-QUANTITATIVE PROTOCOL REASONING)
======================================================================

2.1 Clauses audited (verbatim from V2 Section 7 and A001 Section A6):

    A14_B0= greedy earliest-first over ascending grid_pos; first event
      accepted; subsequent event accepted iff
      pos-last_accepted>=spacing_steps.

    A15_B1_CONTROL= B1 is diagnostic only and uses strict
      pos-last_accepted>spacing_steps. B1 can never replace B0.

    A20_I12= if exact_spacing_pair_n==0 then paired B0 and B1 accepted
      masks, accepted_n and accepted_positions_sha256 are identical.

    A21_EXACT_SPACING_DEFINITION= exact_spacing_pair_n uses consecutive
      pairs in the sorted pre-overlap position array only.

    A001 Section A6:
      exact_spacing_pair_n = count of consecutive pairs in the sorted
      pre-overlap positions array whose difference equals
      spacing_steps.

    Candidate specification Section 7:
      I_HB_I12=if exact_spacing_pair_n==0 then B0 and B1 identical
      per_paired_case

2.2 Synthetic counterexample (synthetic integers only; not derived
    from, and not representative of, any real dataset):

      pre_overlap_positions = [0, 5, 10]
      spacing_steps         = 10

    exact_spacing_pair_n under A21 / A001 Section A6:
      consecutive differences: 5-0=5, 10-5=5; neither equals 10
      exact_spacing_pair_n = 0

    B0 under A14 (>=):
      0  : first event, accepted; last_accepted=0
      5  : 5-0=5,  5>=10 false  -> rejected
      10 : 10-0=10, 10>=10 true -> accepted
      B0 accepted positions = [0, 10]; accepted_n = 2

    B1 under A15 (>):
      0  : first event, accepted; last_accepted=0
      5  : 5-0=5,  5>10 false   -> rejected
      10 : 10-0=10, 10>10 false -> rejected
      B1 accepted positions = [0]; accepted_n = 1

    The premise of A20_I12 holds (exact_spacing_pair_n==0), yet the
    accepted masks differ, accepted_n differs (2 vs 1), and the
    accepted_positions_sha256 values differ (the fingerprinted byte
    strings are the int64 encodings of two and of one position
    respectively, hence distinct inputs). A20_I12 is violated while B0
    and B1 each obey their frozen algorithms exactly.

2.3 General reason. B0 and B1 process the same positions in the same
    order and differ only when a comparison evaluates
    pos-last_accepted == spacing_steps (the only case where >= and >
    disagree). last_accepted need not be the immediate predecessor of
    pos in the pre-overlap array, because intermediate positions may
    have been rejected. The consecutive-pair count of A21 therefore
    does not observe every difference the greedy filters compare, and
    exact_spacing_pair_n==0 does not exclude a B0/B1 divergence.
    A20_I12 is not entailed by A14+A15+A21; the counterexample of 2.2
    proves non-entailment.

2.4 No earlier amendment resolves the inconsistency:
      A001 Section A6 confirms the consecutive-pair definition and
        Section A12 states S2 candidate invariants are unchanged;
      A002 Section B7, A003 Section C4, A004 Section 10.2 and A005
        Section 5.2 each declare overlap semantics, the exact-spacing
        consecutive-pair rule and/or the S2 invariant list unchanged;
      A006 addresses only AMB-R3, provenance, runtime environment,
        session_id uniqueness and manifest JSON duplicate members.
    No clause of A001-A006 overrides, repairs, narrows or otherwise
    resolves A20_I12 or I_HB_I12.

2.5 A19_I11 (B1.accepted_n<=B0.accepted_n) is not affected by this
    finding: every B1 accepted set has all successive gaps
    >spacing_steps, hence >=spacing_steps, so it is a feasible set
    under the B0 constraint, and the greedy earliest-first B0 selection
    has maximum cardinality among such sets. A19_I11 remains entailed
    and remains in force.

3. MATERIALITY
======================================================================

3.1 V2 Section 7 states: all_A01_through_A30_are_fail_closed=true;
    any_A01_through_A30_violation=S2_FAIL.

3.2 V2 Section 15 requires S2_PASS for PASS; otherwise, after a valid
    application execution, the verdict is V1_2_NEW36_PROTOCOL_FAIL.

3.3 Therefore A20_I12 can produce S2_FAIL, and a protocol FAIL, for a
    Candidate that implements B0 and B1 exactly as frozen. The defect
    can change the effective validation PASS/FAIL outcome and is
    material to S2.

3.4 V2 Section 13 forbids any invariant or protocol change once a NEW36
    application invocation begins. The defect must therefore be closed
    before any NEW36 application invocation, and its closure MUST NOT
    depend on inspecting NEW36 to learn whether the pattern of 2.2
    occurs there.

4. NORMATIVE CLAUSES
======================================================================

A007.1 Withdrawal of A20_I12 (R1).
  V2 Section 7 clause A20_I12 is WITHDRAWN as an S2 invariant for this
  V2 validation. A20_I12 MUST NOT be enforced by the Candidate, the
  Reference, the comparator, the validator or any S2 check, and its
  premise or conclusion SHALL NOT produce S2_FAIL. The S2 closed
  invariant list for this V2 validation is A01-A19 and A21-A30;
  remaining clauses keep their V2 identifiers and are not renumbered.
  V2 "all_A01_through_A30_are_fail_closed=true" and
  "any_A01_through_A30_violation=S2_FAIL" apply to those remaining
  clauses only.

A007.2 Withdrawal of I_HB_I12 (R2).
  Candidate specification Section 7 invariant I_HB_I12 is WITHDRAWN for
  this V2 validation. It MUST NOT be treated as an implementation
  conformance requirement for the Candidate or the Reference.

A007.3 A14_B0 preserved (R3).
  A14_B0 is unchanged. B0 remains greedy earliest-first over ascending
  grid_pos; the first event is accepted; a subsequent event is accepted
  iff pos-last_accepted>=spacing_steps. HB=B0 is unchanged.

A007.4 A15_B1_CONTROL preserved (R4).
  A15_B1_CONTROL is unchanged. B1 remains diagnostic only, uses strict
  pos-last_accepted>spacing_steps, and can never replace B0.

A007.5 A21 and A001 Section A6 preserved (R5).
  A21_EXACT_SPACING_DEFINITION and A001 Section A6 are unchanged.
  exact_spacing_pair_n remains the count of consecutive pairs in the
  sorted pre-overlap positions array whose difference equals
  spacing_steps. It is NOT redefined over all unordered pairs; the
  historical Stage3 all-unordered-pairs count remains non-normative as
  stated in A001 Section A6. exact_spacing_pair_n remains an emitted
  field with unchanged definition, type and NULL/COMPUTED status. No
  output field is added, removed or changed; the 25-field row schema is
  unchanged.

A007.6 A19_I11 preserved (R6).
  A19_I11 (paired B1.accepted_n<=B0.accepted_n) remains an S2 invariant
  and remains fail-closed, unchanged.

A007.7 No replacement (R7).
  A007 introduces no replacement for A20_I12 and no replacement for
  I_HB_I12. No new diagnostic, no new predicate, no new S2 check and no
  new output field is introduced, explicitly or implicitly.

A007.8 No other change (R8).
  A007 makes no change to: Candidate or Reference quantitative
  algorithms; thresholds or threshold domains; event selection or
  event eligibility; directions; spacing; overlap filtering;
  metrics; CSV or manifest serialization; the manifest schema or
  manifest contract; the accepted-position fingerprint; AMB-R3
  (A006 Section 4); provenance and runtime gates (A006 Sections 5-8A);
  the NEW36 inventory; S1; S3; S4; S5; the one-shot contract; the
  claim boundary.

A007.9 Precedence (R9).
  Where V2 Section 7 requires A20_I12, or the Candidate specification
  requires I_HB_I12, A007 supersedes only those requirements, for this
  V2 validation. All other clauses of V2, A001, A002, A003, A004, A005,
  A006 and the Candidate specification remain in force with their
  existing precedence. A007 creates no general or residual precedence.
